# Awakening art direction v2

Three coherent anime character models; refined faces, accurate costume construction and clean readable silhouettes. Original action poses remain the motion reference. Nezuko uses a restrained single horn, closed mouth and fully intact costume. Tanjiro keeps stature and strong sword silhouette; Zenitsu uses calm closed eyes. Akaza artwork is preserved byte-for-byte.

Runtime effects are layered behind the fighter and anchored to the current pose. HUD uses a charcoal inset panel, elemental accent, readable title and separately aligned duration.
